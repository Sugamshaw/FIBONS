# Fibons - Bharat-Heritage: Empowering India's Cultural Legacy



## 🌐 Overview

**Fibons - Bharat-Heritage** is a pioneering web-based platform designed to **empower Indian artisans**, preserve traditional craftsmanship, and promote cultural heritage on a **global stage**. Developed during a hackathon, the platform bridges the gap between **local talent** and **global recognition** by offering a robust digital marketplace.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎨 **Artisan Empowerment** – Artisans can register, upload products, and access regional language video guides.
- 🏷️ **Authentic Product Listings** – Products are categorized by state and district, each with **QR-coded certificates**.
- 🌍 **Global Reach** – Supports export-ready integration, enhancing international accessibility.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** – Enables communication and browsing in multiple regional and global languages.
- 🔁 **Sustainable Supply Chain** – Fair compensation and ethical trade for artisans.
- 🧭 **AR/VR Tours** – Immersive experiences to explore heritage sites and artisanal processes.
- 💬 **Interactive Blog** – In-depth cultural insights and stories behind crafts.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js
- **Backend**: Node.js
- **Extended Features**: PlugXR (AR/VR), Multilingual APIs
- **E-Commerce**: Custom product catalog, cart, checkout, user profiles

---

## 🚀 Business Model

To accelerate onboarding, we aim to collaborate with **tour guides** who can identify and assist artisans. The platform targets high-demand export markets like the **USA**, **UK**, **Australia**, **Canada**, and **France**.

---

## 🌍 Market Potential

India’s handloom and handicraft industry has vast export potential. Our platform taps into markets including:
- USA, UK, Australia, Canada
- France, Germany, UAE, Netherlands
- Japan, Switzerland, Italy, LAC regions
